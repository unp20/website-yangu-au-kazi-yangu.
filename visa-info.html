Visa Information

Everything you need to know about getting a visa for Tanzania and Zanzibar.

Visa for Tanzania

Most tourists need a visa to enter Tanzania. You can obtain a visa through two methods:

1. Online Visa (e-Visa)

Visit the official website visa.immigration.go.tz to apply for your visa online. It is fast, easy, and highly recommended.

2. Visa on Arrival

You can obtain a visa upon arrival at the airport in Tanzania. However, please note that this process can take a long time due to queues.

Visa for Zanzibar

Zanzibar is part of Tanzania — therefore, a Tanzanian visa is valid for Zanzibar as well. There is no separate visa required for Zanzibar.

Required Documents

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Passport-sized photograph
  • Return flight ticket
  • Hotel booking confirmation
  • Proof of sufficient funds
  • Completed application form

Visa Tips

  • Apply for your visa early — at least 30 days before your trip.
  • Ensure your passport is valid and not expiring soon.
  • Keep a photocopy of your passport.
  • Always check the official Tanzania Embassy website for updates.

Important Notice

Visa information can change. Please check the official Tanzania Embassy website or visa.immigration.go.tz for the most recent information.
