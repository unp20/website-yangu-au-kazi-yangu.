About Mount Meru

Mount Meru is the second highest mountain in Tanzania (4,566m), located inside Arusha National Park. It is famous for trekking — often used as a warm-up for Kilimanjaro. The mountain is home to a variety of wildlife including buffalos, giraffes, zebras, and many bird species.
