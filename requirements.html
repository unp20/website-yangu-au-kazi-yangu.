1. 🛂 Visa (Entry Permit)

Most tourists need a visa to enter Tanzania. You can obtain a visa through two methods:

A. Online Visa (e-Visa) ⭐ Recommended

Official Website: visa.immigration.go.tz

Apply for your visa online. It is fast, easy, and safe.

  • Apply 30 days before your trip
  • Pay by card (Visa/Mastercard)
  • Receive your visa via email
  • Print it and bring it with you

B. Visa on Arrival

You can obtain a visa upon arrival at the airport in Tanzania. However, this can take a long time due to queues.

2. 🛡️ Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is highly recommended for all tourists. It protects you against:

  • Health emergencies: Accidents, illness, hospital
  • Trip cancellation: Lose of money
  • Lost luggage: Flights, safaris
  • Flight emergencies: Flight cancellation
  • Property damage: Camera, phone

Recommended Companies

  • World Nomads — Popular for travelers
  • Allianz Travel — Global insurance
  • AXA Travel — Good insurance
  • SafetyWing — Budget for travelers
  • Travel Guard — Comprehensive insurance

Insurance Costs

  • 7 Days: $20 - $50 USD
  • 14 Days: $40 - $80 USD
  • 30 Days: $80 - $150 USD
  • 1 Year: $200 - $500 USD

4. 🟡 Yellow Fever — Required Certificate

Very Important

If you are coming from a country with yellow fever, you need a yellow fever vaccination certificate. Without it — you may be denied entry into Tanzania.

  • Get vaccinated 2 weeks before your trip
  • Get an International Certificate
  • Bring it with you — it will be checked at the airport
  • The certificate is valid for 10 years
