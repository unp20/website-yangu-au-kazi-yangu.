About Mountain Hiking

Mountain hiking is the perfect way to relax, enjoy nature, and learn about local cultures. Tanzania is home to some of the most beautiful mountains for hiking — suitable for everyone, from beginners to experts.

Hiking Tips

  • ✓ Train physically 2-4 weeks in advance
  • ✓ Wear sturdy hiking boots
  • ✓ Pack warm clothing (layers)
  • ✓ Drink plenty of water
  • ✓ Always hike with an experienced guide
  • ✓ Carry altitude sickness medication
  • ✓ Bring a camera
  • ✓ Enjoy every step of the journey!

Essential Gear

  • 🎒 Hiking boots / sturdy shoes
  • 🧥 Warm, layered clothing
  • 🧢 Hat and sunglasses
  • 💧 Plenty of drinking water
  • 🍫 High-energy snacks
  • 📷 Camera
  • 🩹 First-aid kit
  • 🎒 Backpack
