Lake Manyara National Park

Flamingos, ndege wengi, na wanyama — kaskazini mwa Tanzania.

Kuhusu Lake Manyara

Lake Manyara National Park ni mbuga iliyopo Manyara, Tanzania. Ni maarufu kwa flamingos wengi, ndege 400+ aina, na wanyama. Ni mahali pazuri kwa bird watching na kuona wanyama. Pia ni nyumbani kwa simba wanaopanda miti (tree-climbing lions).
