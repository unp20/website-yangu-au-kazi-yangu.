🗓️ Best Time to Visit

🌞 June - October

Dry season. Perfect for safaris, beaches, and hiking. The best time to visit Tanzania.

🌤️ December - February

Short dry season. Hot and sunny. Great for beaches and wildlife viewing.

🌧️ April - May

Long rainy season. Avoid travel during this period. Many hotels close.

🌦️ November

Short rains. Good for budget travel. Some rain but less crowds.
