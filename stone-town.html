Stone Town

The historic heart of Zanzibar — a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

About Stone Town

Stone Town (Mji Mkongwe) is the historic old city of Zanzibar. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site due to its rich history, unique architecture, and blend of Arab, African, Indian, and European cultures. It is the perfect place to walk around and learn about the island's history.

Attractions in Stone Town

🌳

Forodhani Gardens

Beautiful gardens and a famous night food market.

🏰

Old Fort

A historic Arab fortress built in the 17th century.

🏛️

House of Wonders

A famous historic building and museum.

👑

Palace Museum

The historic palace of the Sultans of Zanzibar.

⛪

Anglican Cathedral

A historic cathedral built on the site of the old slave market.

🕊️

Slave Market

A memorial site dedicated to the history of the slave trade.

🛒

Darajani Market

The main bustling market of the city.

🎤

Freddie Mercury House

The birthplace of the legendary Queen frontman.
