Cancellation Policy

Our refund and cancellation terms.

1. Cancellation by Customer

If you need to cancel your booking, the following refund policy applies:

  • 30+ days before trip: 100% refund
  • 15-29 days before trip: 75% refund
  • 7-14 days before trip: 50% refund
  • 3-6 days before trip: 25% refund
  • 0-2 days before trip: No refund

2. Cancellation by UNP Tours

In the rare event that UNP Tours must cancel your trip (due to weather, safety concerns, or other reasons), you will receive a 100% refund or the option to reschedule.

3. Changes to Bookings

Changes made 3+ days in advance are free of charge. Later changes may incur additional fees depending on availability.

4. No-Show Policy

If you do not show up for your scheduled trip without prior notice, no refund will be issued.
