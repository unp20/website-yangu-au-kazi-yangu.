About Serengeti

Serengeti National Park is a world-famous park located in northern Tanzania. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, renowned for the Great Migration — millions of animals migrate every year. It is the ultimate destination for an authentic African safari.

The Great Migration

The Great Migration is the annual movement of millions of animals (wildebeest, zebra, gazelle) from the Serengeti to the Masai Mara (Kenya) and back. It is one of the most spectacular wildlife events on Earth.
