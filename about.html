About UNP TOURS

Unique & Natural Paradise — Zanzibar's premier luxury tour operator.

🌴 Our Story

Founded in Nungwi, Zanzibar by Fe Brooks Irie, UNP Tours was born from a passion to share the magic of Zanzibar with the world. What started as a small local operation has grown into a premier tour operator offering luxury excursions, safaris, water sports, and unforgettable cultural experiences.

Our name — Unique & Natural Paradise — reflects our commitment to providing authentic, one-of-a-kind experiences that celebrate the natural beauty and rich heritage of Zanzibar and Tanzania.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver world-class luxury travel experiences that exceed expectations while supporting local communities and preserving the environment.

👁️

Our Vision

To become Zanzibar's most trusted and innovative tour operator, connecting travelers with the soul of the island.

💎

Our Values

Authenticity, excellence, sustainability, and a deep love for Zanzibar's culture and people.
