Travel Tips

Everything you need to know before visiting Zanzibar.

🌡️

Best Time to Visit

June-October is the dry season, perfect for safaris and beaches. December-February is hot and sunny. Avoid April-May (rainy season).

💉

Health & Vaccinations

Yellow fever vaccine required if coming from endemic countries. Malaria prophylaxis recommended. Drink bottled water only.

👕

What to Pack

Light cotton clothes, sunscreen, hat, swimwear, comfortable walking shoes, insect repellent, and modest clothing for Stone Town.

💰

Money & Currency

Tanzanian Shilling (TZS) is the local currency. USD widely accepted. ATMs available in Stone Town. Carry cash for small purchases.

📱

SIM Cards & Internet

Buy local SIM (Vodacom, Airtel) at airport. 4G coverage good in tourist areas. Most hotels have WiFi.

🙏

Culture & Etiquette

Zanzibar is 99% Muslim. Dress modestly in Stone Town. Ask before taking photos of locals. Respect Ramadan traditions.

🚗

Getting Around

Taxis and daladalas (local buses) available. Rent a scooter or car for flexibility. Book airport transfers through UNP Tours.

🛡️

Safety

Zanzibar is generally safe. Avoid walking alone at night. Use hotel safes. Book tours through licensed operators like UNP Tours.
