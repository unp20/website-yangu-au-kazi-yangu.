About Jambiani

Jambiani is a traditional fishing village located on the east coast of Zanzibar, near Paje. It is famous for its peaceful, untouched beaches, the warm local Swahili culture, and unique experiences. It is the perfect destination for travelers looking to relax and immerse themselves in authentic island life.

Attractions in Jambiani

🏖️

Jambiani Beach

A peaceful beach with powdery white sand and crystal-clear waters.

🛖

Swahili Culture

Experience the authentic daily life and traditions of a local fishing village.

Seaweed Farming

Learn about the traditional seaweed farming done by local women.
