Kuhusu Kilimanjaro

Mount Kilimanjaro ni mlima mrefu Afrika (5,895m). Ipo Moshi, Tanzania. Ni maarufu kwa kupanda — watu elfu nyingi wanapanda kila mwaka. Kuna njia 7 za kupanda:

  • Marangu Route — Rahisi, huts
  • Machame Route — Nzuri, camps
  • Lemosho Route — Nzuri sana, camps
  • Rongai Route — Kutoka Kenya, camps
  • Shira Route — Nzuri, camps
  • Umbwe Route — Ngumu, camps
  • Northern Circuit — Nzuri sana, camps
