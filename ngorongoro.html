About Ngorongoro

The Ngorongoro Crater is the world's largest inactive, intact volcanic caldera, located in northern Tanzania. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous for its incredible concentration of wildlife including lions, elephants, rhinos, and more. It is the perfect place for a safari and getting up close to nature.
