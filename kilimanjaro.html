Mount Kilimanjaro

The highest mountain in Africa (5,895m) — Uhuru Peak and 7 incredible trekking routes.

About Kilimanjaro

Mount Kilimanjaro is the highest mountain in Africa, standing at 5,895m. Located in Moshi, Tanzania, it is world-famous for trekking — thousands of people climb it every year. The summit is known as "Uhuru Peak" and it is one of the Seven Summits (the highest mountains on each continent).

Trekking Tips

  • ✓ Train physically 4-6 weeks in advance
  • ✓ Pack warm clothes (freezing at summit)
  • ✓ Drink plenty of water
  • ✓ Rest well and acclimatize
  • ✓ Always trek with an experienced guide
  • ✓ Carry altitude sickness medication
  • ✓ Eat high-energy foods
  • ✓ Enjoy every step of the journey

How to Get There

  • ✈️
    By Air: Fly from Dar es Salaam or Zanzibar to Kilimanjaro Airport (JRO).
  • 🚗
    By Road: Drive from Arusha (approx. 2 hours).
  • ⛴️
    From Zanzibar: Take a flight from Zanzibar to Kilimanjaro Airport.
