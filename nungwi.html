About Nungwi

Nungwi is a fishing village located on the northern tip of Zanzibar. It is famous for its stunning beaches (often ranked among the best in the world), crystal-clear turquoise waters, and lively nightlife. It is the perfect place to relax, swim, and soak up the sun.
