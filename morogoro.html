Morogoro

Discover the beauty of Morogoro — Mikumi, Udzungwa, mountains, and rich culture.

About Morogoro

Morogoro is a vibrant tourism town located in eastern Tanzania (195 km from Dar es Salaam). It is famous for Mikumi National Park, the Udzungwa Mountains, and the rich cultures of the Wapare, Wazigua, and Washambaa people. It is the perfect destination for mountain tourism and safaris.

Attractions in Morogoro

🦁

Mikumi National Park

A famous safari park near Morogoro — lions, elephants, giraffes, and more.

🏔️

Udzungwa Mountains

Beautiful mountains featuring Sanje Waterfall, hiking trails, and unique primates.

🏙️

Morogoro Town

A charming town with a bustling local market.

🌿

Kilombero Valley

Breathtaking landscapes in the Kilombero Valley.

🎭

Cultural Tourism

Experience the authentic cultures of the Wapare, Wazigua, and Washambaa.

⛰️

Mwanihana Peak

A rewarding hike to the summit of Mwanihana.

Mountain Cabin Tourism

Morogoro is famous for mountain tourism. Visitors come to stay in cabins and camps in the mountains. This is called "Mountain Cabin Tourism" — a unique experience to relax in the highlands.

  • 🏡 Udzungwa Cabins
  • 🏡 Mikumi Cabins
  • 🏡 Morogoro Cabins
  • 🏡 Kilombero Cabins
