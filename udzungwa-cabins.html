Udzungwa Cabins

Mountain lodges and camps in the Udzungwa Mountains National Park — home of waterfalls and unique primates.

About Udzungwa Mountains

Udzungwa Mountains National Park is a mountain park located in Morogoro, Tanzania. It is renowned for its unique wildlife, rare birds, and spectacular waterfalls. It is a paradise for hiking and nature walks.

Attractions

  • 💧 Sanje Waterfall (170m drop)
  • 💧 Prince Bernhard Waterfall
  • 🥾 Hiking Trails
  • 🐒 Unique Primates (Monkeys)
  • 🦜 Bird Watching
  • ⛰️ Mwanihana Peak

Tips for Visitors

  • ✓ Bring comfortable hiking clothes
  • ✓ Wear sturdy hiking shoes
  • ✓ Carry plenty of drinking water
  • ✓ Always walk with an experienced guide
  • ✓ Enjoy the waterfalls
  • ✓ Take lots of photos!
